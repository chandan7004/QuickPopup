Quick Popup Search

Open anything. Lose nothing. Return instantly.

Some shortcuts aren't set. Another extension or Chrome may already use them.

Your shortcuts (work anywhere in Chrome)

Change any of them: chrome://extensions/shortcuts

The flow

  1. Press ⌘⇧K, type so genserver timeout, press ↵ → opens in a popup window.
  2. Press ⌘⇧K again → back to your tab. The popup stays open.
  3. Press it again → the popup comes forward. With 2+ popups you get a switcher.
  4. Want to keep a popup safe from being replaced? Pin it.
  5. Page deserves a real tab? Move it into a tab. It keeps its place: scroll, forms, video.
  6. ⌘W closes the popup and returns you to your tab. Closed and replaced pages stay in “Recently closed”.

Popups are normal Chrome windows: they don't stay on top. When one is hidden behind your tab, the shortcut brings it straight back.

In the search box

↵Open in a new popup. (Setting: replace the last popup instead. The preview line then shows what gets replaced.)
⌘↵The other one (replace last popup / new popup)
⇧↵Normal tab
↑ ↓Pick from open popups, recent searches, recently closed
